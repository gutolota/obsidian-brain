# Daily planning policy

## Scope
Default detailed window: 14 days of `Diário/`. Also include any older daily note with an unchecked task.

## One source of truth
Tasks remain in their source daily or project note. `Sistema/Agenda e prazos.md` aggregates and proposes; it does not duplicate commitments back into notes.

## Date classes
1. **Explicit structured**: Tasks-plugin due/scheduled/start date markers.
2. **Explicit prose**: “entregar até 12/09”, “prazo sexta”. Preserve the phrase and resolve only when unambiguous from the note date.
3. **Suggested**: agent recommendation based on workload, meetings, dependencies, or recency. Always pending user approval.
4. **Ambiguous**: cannot be resolved safely. Ask or list under `Datas a confirmar`.

## Suggestions
Suggestions may prioritize, split an oversized task, surface a dependency, recommend a review, or propose a date. Each suggestion cites source notes and explains the reason in one sentence. Never present a suggestion as a user commitment.

## Completion
Only checked boxes are counted as completed. A prose statement that something was done may be summarized as evidence, but does not mutate a checkbox.
